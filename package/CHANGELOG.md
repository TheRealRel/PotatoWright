# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release: Ask and Embla, wooden mannequins in the game's 15 stances and all 25 emotes, with a colour and base panel and their own fold-open entry in the build menu; and ropes and iron, bronze and black metal chains that hang between any two points, up to 16 m, with the sag set by the mouse wheel. Ropes and chains work on ships with Build On Ship.
