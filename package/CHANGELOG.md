# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- New: scaffolding. Frames stack, step up as stairs and open into each other; the top one has a guard rail, and every third step up a stair stands on long legs. Frames fall when what holds them goes.
- New: things to hang on ropes and chains: Dvergr, hooded and Deep North lanterns, a hanging brazier, drying herbs and pennant bunting.
- Ropes and chains now reach 48 m, or 64 m with the new server setting "Ropes and chains: longest". Long ones keep their full detail.
- New pictures on the mod page.

## 1.0.0
- First release: Ask and Embla, wooden mannequins in the game's 15 stances and all 25 emotes, with a colour and base panel and their own fold-open entry in the build menu; and ropes and iron, bronze and black metal chains that hang between any two points, up to 16 m, with the sag set by the mouse wheel. Ropes and chains work on ships with Build On Ship.
